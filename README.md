## 📌 About The Project

**The Wipeout Project** is a free, privacy-first, client-side web tool designed to help Muslims engage in structured self-accountability (*Muhasabah*) and good deed redemption (*Tawbah*). 

The application generates clean, high-contrast **printable checkbox sheets** tailored for long-term goal tracking (spanning years or decades). Each checked box represents **one completed act of Ibadah** (such as voluntary prayers-*Nafl Salah*, *Sadaqah*, *Istighfar*, or fasting) performed to build a mountain of good deeds and erase past shortfalls.

This project is built upon the divine promise in the Holy Qur'an:

> *"Indeed, good deeds wipe out bad ones. That is a reminder for those who remember."*  
> — **Surah Hud (11:114)**


## 📜 Islamic Principles & Etiquette

When using this tool for personal accounting, users are strongly advised to adhere to the core Islamic principles of repentance:

### 1. Complete Secrecy (*Sitr*)
If a shortfall or sin is **strictly personal between you and Allah** and **does not harm or affect anyone else**, Islam strongly encourages keeping it concealed while performing sincere repentance (*Tawbah*) and good deeds.

* **How this app protects your privacy:**
  * **Zero Cloud Storage / Client-Side Only:** All calculations run entirely inside your local browser memory. No data is saved, tracked, or sent to any server.
  * **Anonymous Task Titles:** The generator uses abstract codes (`Purification 01`, `Purification 02`, etc.). The specific misdeed is never typed, stored, or printed, keeping your private accounting strictly between you and Allah.

### 2. Focus on Hope (*Raja'*)
Counting shortfalls should never lead to despair in Allah's mercy. The purpose of *Muhasabah* is to motivate immediate repentance and energetic good action.

> *"Say, 'O My servants who have transgressed against themselves [by sinning], do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful."*  
> — **Surah Az-Zumar (39:53)**


## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Private Mental Calculation:** Privately calculate your required target of good deeds on a scrap pad or in your mind, then assign each to an anonymous slot (`Purification 01`, `Purification 02`, etc.).
2. **Enter Parameters:**
   - **Start & End Year:** Enter your timeframe (between 1900 and 2300).
   - **Number of Items:** Select how many purification goals to track (1 to 20).
   - **Target Amounts:** Assign the total target for each item (max 20,000 units per item).
3. **Generate & Print:** Click **Generate Preview & Printable Sheets**, then click **🖨️ Print This Document**.


## ✨ Features

- **Automatic Yearly Distribution:** Calculates total targets across multi-year spans (e.g., 2020–2026) and automatically divides the checkboxes evenly per year.
- **Yearly Quote Header:** Displays Umar ibn al-Khattab's famous reminder on accountability at the top of every new year section.
- **Exact 15-Column Print Layout:** Designed specifically for standard A4 portrait printing with crisp, high-contrast black-and-white styling.
